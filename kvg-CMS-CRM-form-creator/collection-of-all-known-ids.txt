# ClickDimensions Form ID Inventory

This inventory covers identifiers used by the generated forms and the older
form samples in this project. In the field tables, **CRM field name** is the
submitted HTML `name` value; **HTML id** is the corresponding DOM `id` value.
The CRM field name is the value that must match the ClickDimensions field.

these as new field mappings.

## Scope Notes

- HTML IDs used only by the form-builder interface (for example `formId` or
	`next-btn`) are not included; they are not emitted as form fields.
- Display labels, autocomplete tokens, option values, section-heading IDs, and
	privacy-policy URLs are not CRM/form identifiers and are not listed here.
- `PASTE-FORM-ID-HERE` is a generator fallback placeholder, not a known
	ClickDimensions Form Capture ID.
